Do not create, edit, run, or delete any tasks.

8. One anonymized representative output, if available; otherwise clearly label an illustrative output.
9. What works well, recurring failures, manual effort, and features I have asked to improve. Use only evidence you can access.
10. The smallest useful version worth rebuilding for students and recent graduates as a local course-project MVP. Put networking, recruiter discovery, essay assistance, and interview/OA tracking in a separate optional section, unless I explicitly confirm otherwise.

Finish with a concise list of unknowns and information I should provide. Omit credentials, personal contact details, private resume content, and identifiable recruiter or employer correspondence.
